TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION 
A- Floor Assembly: 
A1-: Base Frame: 
I-Beam 140 mm spaced according to the requirement of load distribution. 
Two Lifting Points at each side. 
Cleaned, primed and painted with anti rust paint. 
A2-: Floor Deck Frame: 
40 x 40 steel tube 1.5 mm thick @ 40 cm center to center. 
A3-: Floor Deck: 
Floor deck with 18 mm plywood sheets. 
A4-: Floor Covering: 
1.5 mm thick vinyl tiles in Mass hall area & Accommodation floor covered with 
carpet. 
A5-: Wet Areas Floor Covering: 
1.5 mm thick Vinyl sheet for wet flooring areas with welded joints. 
B-Walls Assembly 
B1-:Exterior Walls: 
External wall made with 50 mm Ready House panel system. 
All Openings for doors, Windows and air-conditioning are properly framed and 
reinforced and meet the design criteria. 
External wall covered with 0.35 mm thick pre-painted low rib corrugated sheets. 
Internal wall finish with 4 mm thick decorative plywood. 
B2-:Internal Walls: 
Internal wall made with 50 mm thick Ready House panel system. 
Both side covered with 4 mm thick decorative plywood. Kitchen & 
toilet wall made with water proof panel system. 
C- Roof Assembly: 
C1-: Roof Structure 
40 x 40 & 80 x 40 steel tube 1.5 mm thick. 
0.35 pre-printed off- White High Rib corrugated sheet. 
D-Ceiling Assembly: 
Accommodation Ceiling made with 6 mm plywood white color sheets, 
All offices ceiling made with 600 x 600 tiles. 
50 mm thick insulation layer of fiber Glass wool between joists 
Clear Ceiling Height is 2420 mm. 
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION 
E-External Doors: 
900 x 2000 mm single Leaf with 1.6 mm Aluminum profile. 
F- Interior Door: 
900 x 2000 mm doors with aluminum frame, both side 
covered with decorative plywood 
for rooms. 
650 x 2000 mm single Leaf doors for toilets. 
G- Windows: 
100 x 100 mm aluminum window with 6 mm clear glass 
horizontal sliding type with removable fly screen Mesh. 
H- Electrical: 
H1-: Power Supply: 
220 v, 60 Hz, Load Centers 3 phase. 
Main Distribution panel board with Main Breaker. 
H2-: Lighting: 
2 x 40 W florescent light fixture for living rooms. 
1 x 20 W florescent lights with diffuser for toilet area. 
H3-: Receptacles: 
220 v outlets, standard MK type for circuit outlets. 
220 v outlets, Standard MK type for A/c units. 
H4-: Wiring: 
Copper romex cable to all devices and fixers. 
H5-: others: 
All Bathrooms/Toilets include exhaust fans. 
All exterior doors include weatherproof light fixture above. 
All electrical switches, & sockets MK brand. 
I-Plumbing: 
Cold Water supply piping PVC Schedule 40. 
Hot Water supply piping CPVC schedule 80. 
Drain pipe PVC schedule 20. 
Accessories include mixtures, Mirrors, T.P holders, soap dish. 
Common Toilet Area include Eastern or Western type toilet & Wash Basin.
